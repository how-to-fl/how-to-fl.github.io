Cohort 6's list, grouped by where they are, because in Beijing the neighbourhood decides the
evening: what you can walk between, and how long the ride home is.

**Every name below opens in Amap.** That is the useful bit — tap it on your phone and you
have navigation and the Chinese address without typing anything. The prices are the
per-person averages Amap shows, which are a fair guide to what an evening costs rather than
a promise.

What's thin here is what each place is actually *like*. These came off a recommendations
sheet as names, and we'd rather list them honestly than invent atmosphere for them. If
you've been, that sentence is the most valuable thing you can add.

KTV is worth doing once early. You book a room by the hour rather than buying a ticket, the
songbook has plenty of English, and it is the single easiest way to spend an evening with
Chinese classmates without needing much Chinese.

## Live music

**[TributePub 致敬酒吧](https://uri.amap.com/marker?position=116.471702,39.949728&name=TributePub%20%E8%87%B4%E6%95%AC%E9%85%92%E5%90%A7&src=howtofl&coordinate=gaode)** — the one to go
to for live music. It's out on Chaoyang Park Road in Maizidian, east of Sanlitun, so it's a
trip of its own rather than somewhere you wander into on the way home. It's on
[the map](/map/).

Both breweries above put bands on too. What we still don't keep is a list of *what's worth
going to* — if you see something good, that's the bit to add.

## Getting home late

The subway stops before you'll want it to — last trains are roughly 22:30–23:30 depending on
the line. **Didi is the answer**, it's genuinely cheap split between three or four, and it's
the app you [set up before you flew](/china/before-you-fly/#the-checklist).

Two things worth knowing at 2am: have the **Chinese address of Global Village** ready to show
the driver — it's on [the map](/map/), with a copy button — and agree who's paying before you
order, because splitting a Didi afterwards is more annoying than it sounds.

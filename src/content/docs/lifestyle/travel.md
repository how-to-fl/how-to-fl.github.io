China is enormous, the trains are excellent, and the breaks are long. This is the part of
the year people regret not using.

## Book it on Trip.com

**Trip.com / Ctrip (携程)** books trains, hotels and flights in one place, and the reason to
start there rather than anywhere else is blunt: **the English interface is genuinely easy to
navigate**. Most of the alternatives assume you read Chinese.

It's the practical answer to "how do I get to Shanghai for the weekend" — one app, three
bookings, in a language you read.

Rough costs are on [what the year costs](/china/what-it-costs/#travelling): high-speed rail
to Shanghai around €120, a flight somewhere further like Chengdu around €100, hotels from
€10–30 a night depending on the city.

:::caution[Before you leave the country]
Your residence permit may be single-entry. **Convert it to multiple-entry before you book
anything international**, and allow up to two weeks — see
[visas and permits](/china/visas-and-permits/#going-multiple-entry-before-you-travel). Take
your passport for domestic travel too; you need it for tickets and hotel check-in.
:::

**Meituan (美团)** is the main food delivery app, and it does a great deal more than food.
It's one of the two or three apps that make daily life here work.

## Check Taobao before you order

This is the trick nobody tells you, and it's worth real money.

**Before you order from a restaurant on Meituan, search for it on
[Taobao](/misc/taobao/) first.** Restaurants sell vouchers and set-meal deals there — the
same food, from the same kitchen, often at a significant discount. You buy the voucher on
Taobao and redeem it on the order.

It takes an extra minute and it is not a small saving. Worth making a habit of it for
anywhere you order from regularly.

## Finding somewhere in the first place

Meituan is for *ordering*. **Dianping (大众点评)** is for *deciding* — it's the app people
actually use to find somewhere, read reviews and book a table. The two are from the same
company and share a lot of data, but Dianping is the better front door.

Dianping is also good well beyond restaurants: cafés, activities, haircuts, nails, massage.
If you're trying to find any kind of local service and don't know where to start, start
there.

Shared bikes through Meituan are yellow, cost almost nothing, and are the fastest way across
Haidian — see [sports and getting around](/pku/sports-and-extracurriculars/#running-and-cycling).
You unlock them by scanning the QR code on the bike.

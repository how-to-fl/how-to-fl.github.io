These are Cohort 6's, collected over the autumn of 2025. They're first-hand — somebody put
their name to each one on our recommendations sheet, which is a better guarantee than a
five-star average from strangers.

It's a short list because it's an honest one. Add yours.

## Near campus

**BRBR 叙利亚餐厅 — Syrian.** Wraps and sandwiches, and the two to order are the **chicken
shawarma** and the **falafel**. Roughly 2 km east of the east gate, in Zhongguancun, and it's
on Meituan if you'd rather it came to you.

**汗巴巴巴基斯坦餐厅 — Pakistani.** The **naan and the butter chicken**, together, were
described on our sheet as "an insane combo", and we're not going to improve on that. Also
Meituan.

Both of these are takeaway-first, which matters more than it sounds in January.

## Regional Chinese food

**小滇娘 Xiaodianniang — Yunnan.** Two must-orders: **油焖鸡**, braised chicken with potato,
mildly spicy; and **乳扇 rushan**, a Yunnan dairy thing that exists almost nowhere else in
China and is worth trying precisely because it's strange.
[On Dianping](https://www.dianping.com/shop/872619265/photos).

Yunnan food is a good default when you want Chinese food that isn't the same Chinese food —
it's herby and sour and mild rather than heavy.

## When you need something that isn't Chinese

**Pho13** — South-East Asian. Vietnamese, and the thing you want when you have a cold.

The Syrian and Pakistani places above also count. Around month three, most of us hit a point
where we'd have paid real money for bread, and it's useful to know in advance where it is.

## Worth crossing the city for

**Sanlitun — 太古里 Taikoo Li, and 国贸 Guomao.** Outdoor and indoor malls respectively, and
the default answer to "where do we go on Saturday". Taikoo Li is where the bars and the
people-watching are; Guomao is the indoor, air-conditioned, actually-pleasant-in-August
version. It's a proper trip across the city from campus — see
[the map](/map/) before you set off.

**Kocoon Spa** — spa and waxing, and the reason it's on this list rather than a search
result: **they speak English**. About an hour from Global Village and pricier than the local
alternative, and you get tea and biscuits afterwards. Worth it for the appointments where you
really don't want a language barrier.
[Their site](https://www.kocoonspalounge.com/pages/waxing).

## Finding somewhere yourself

The app for this is **Dianping (大众点评)** — reviews, photos and table reservations, and it
goes well beyond restaurants into cafés, activities and services. It's the front door;
[Meituan](/lifestyle/meituan/) is for ordering once you've decided.

And before you order delivery from anywhere: **check [Taobao](/misc/taobao/) for a voucher
first.** Same restaurant, same food, frequently cheaper.

## Cafés and places to work

On campus, the coffee is [in the canteens list](/pku/canteens/#coffee) — including which
building each one is in, which is what you actually need between classes.

Off campus, **[Cafe Zarah](https://uri.amap.com/marker?position=116.406208,39.940745&name=Cafe%20Zarah&src=howtofl&coordinate=gaode)**
on Gulou East Street (about ¥56) is the one that comes up most, and it doubles as the start
of a hutong evening — see [going out](/lifestyle/nightlife/).

**[Plato Cafe](https://uri.amap.com/marker?position=116.325353,39.992827&name=Plato%20Cafe%20%28Lanqiying%29&src=howtofl&coordinate=gaode)** — Chengfu Road 69, on the
second floor over in Tsinghua's Lanqiying, about ¥44. Cohort 6's answer to this exact
question: **a nice place to spend the day**, which is the bit you can't tell from a listing.

More of those, please — which café tolerates a laptop for three hours, and which one has
plugs, is precisely what this page is for.

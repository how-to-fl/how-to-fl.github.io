Beijing has a proper continental climate: a long cold winter, a long hot summer, and
**spring and autumn that are lovely and over in about three weeks each**. Plan the trips you
care about for those windows.

<ul class="fl-stats">
  <li><span class="n">−10 °C</span><span class="l">winter lows, and it's a dry cold</span></li>
  <li><span class="n">36 °C</span><span class="l">summer highs, humid</span></li>
  <li><span class="n">AQI 100+</span><span class="l">where a mask starts being worth it</span></li>
</ul>

## The year, month by month

**September** — you arrive. Warm, bright, the best weather you'll get. Orientation, the
course add/drop scramble, and PKU runs trips in the first two weeks (Great Wall, Forbidden
City) — go on them, that's where you meet everyone.

**Late September** — the **Club Fest**, 100+ societies, near PKU Hall and the New Sun
Student Center. Genuinely one of the best afternoons of the year.

**Early October** — **Golden Week.** No classes. The entire country travels at once, so
either book weeks ahead or deliberately stay in a quiet Beijing, which is its own pleasure.

**November** — cold arrives fast, heating goes on, air quality starts to matter. This is
when the air purifier earns its keep.

**December** — **no Christmas break.** Term continues as normal. Worth telling your family
early, and worth making plans with whoever else is staying, because it's the month that
catches people out emotionally.

**January** — exam period, then the winter break begins.

**Late January / February** — **Chinese New Year.** The date moves with the lunar calendar,
which is why the winter break is 4–6 weeks rather than a fixed length. Campus empties
almost completely. If you can travel with a Chinese friend to their home, do — it's the
single best cultural experience on offer, and invitations are common.

**March** — spring semester, and the second club recruitment round.

**Early May** — **May break.** No classes.

**June** — exams, then the summer break, 8–10 weeks from late June.

## Air quality

It's better than its reputation and much better than it was ten years ago, but it isn't
nothing.

- Check an AQI app the way you'd check the weather
- **Above roughly 100–150, wear a mask.** Below that most people don't bother
- **Get an air purifier for your room.** Xiaomi ones are cheap on JD, they work, and you can
  sell it to the next cohort when you leave — everyone does

Bad days cluster in winter. Some weeks are flawless blue sky.

## What to bring for it

- A **proper winter coat**. −10 °C with wind is not a "layer up" situation
- Something for **36 °C and humid**
- Both of these, given spring and autumn barely happen

**Taobao (淘宝)** is where you furnish your room, and it is cheaper than carrying anything
from home. It is also, less obviously, where you buy your dinner cheaper.

## The restaurant voucher trick

Worth knowing before anything else on this page, because it saves money every week rather
than once.

**Restaurants sell vouchers and set-meal deals on Taobao** — the same food, from the same
kitchen you were about to order from on [Meituan](/lifestyle/meituan/), often at a
significant discount. Search the restaurant on Taobao first, buy the voucher, then redeem it.

An extra minute per order, and a real saving on anywhere you eat regularly.

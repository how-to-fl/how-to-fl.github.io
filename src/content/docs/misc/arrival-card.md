**Print this page before you fly.** The moment you'll want it most is standing outside the
airport with no SIM, no data and a taxi driver waiting — which is precisely the moment you
can't load a website.

Printing it gives you just the card below; the rest of this page won't appear.

## What's deliberately not on it

Anything that changes. Your flight details, your room number, your dorm building — write
those on the back in pen, because they're different for everyone and they're the bits you'll
actually forget.

If you'd rather have the official version, the FL team's *Get Ready for PKU* booklet ends
with a print-out page of the same kind, with a few more phrases on it.

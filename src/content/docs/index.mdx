## 欢迎 — welcome

Beijing is a city of twenty-two million people where your phone, your bank card and your
usual way of finding dinner all work differently. Most of us spent the first fortnight
quietly confused, and a fair few of us are still working bits of it out.

So we're writing it down as we go.

This isn't official and nobody from the programme office checks it. It's ours — Cohort 6,
Cohort 7 and whoever comes after — and it's only as good as what we bother to add. Which is
why **every page says when one of us last checked it**, and says so loudly when that was
ages ago. If you know something this page doesn't, that includes you.

## Tell us what you know

The best person to explain the first week is whoever had it most recently, and the best
person to explain the rest of the year is whoever is in the middle of it. If something threw
you — last week or last September — **that is the most useful thing you own right now**,
because you'll have forgotten it was ever confusing within a fortnight.

## Who this is for

Students on the **Future Leaders International Undergraduate Double Degree Program**
at Peking University — Guanghua's flagship international undergraduate initiative,
now a network of 15 partner business schools.

Cohort 7 lands in September. Cohort 6 is already here. This site is written for both,
and for every cohort after.

## How to trust this

Every page says when it was last checked. Anything older than a year gets a visible
warning at the top, because restaurants close, visa rules change and apps get
redesigned — and a guide you can trust is worth more than a guide that is merely
comprehensive.

If something here is wrong, [it takes two minutes to say so](/start/how-to-use-this/).

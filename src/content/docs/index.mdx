## 欢迎 — welcome

You're about to move to a city of twenty-two million people, where your phone, your bank
card and your usual way of finding dinner all work differently. We know, because we did it
too, and most of us spent the first fortnight quietly confused.

So we wrote it all down for you.

This isn't official and nobody from the programme office checks it. It's us — Cohorts 6 and
7 and whoever comes next — passing on what we learned the hard way. Which also means it's
only as good as what we bother to write down, so **every page tells you when one of us last
checked it**, and shouts at you if that was ages ago.

## Tell us what you know

The best person to explain the first week is someone who just had it. If you've just landed
and something threw you, **that confusion is the most useful thing you own right now** — and
you'll have forgotten it was ever confusing within a fortnight.

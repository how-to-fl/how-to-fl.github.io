## 欢迎 — welcome to Beijing

You are about to move to a city of twenty-two million people, in a country where your
phone, your bank card and your usual way of finding a restaurant all work differently.
Roughly two hundred people have already done exactly this, on exactly this program.

This site is what they learned, written down before they forgot it.

It is not official. It is written by students in Cohorts 6 and 7 and everyone after,
and it is only as good as what we bother to write down — which is why **every page
tells you when it was last checked**, and says so loudly when that was too long ago.

## Add what you know

The person best placed to explain the first week is someone who just had it. If you have
just arrived and something confused you, **that confusion is the most valuable thing on
this site** — and it has a shelf life of about a fortnight before you forget it was ever
confusing.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="If GitHub loads for you" icon="github">
    Click the pencil at the bottom of any page to edit it, or fill in a short form to add
    a place to the map. Five fields, no code, no pull request.

    [Open a form →](https://github.com/how-to-fl/how-to-fl.github.io/issues/new/choose)
  </Card>

  <Card title="If GitHub does not load" icon="comment">
    From a Beijing connection it often won't, and that is not your problem to solve.
    Send it by WeChat or email, or bring it to the next contribution sprint — someone
    will type it in, and your name still goes on it.

    [How that works →](/how-to-use-this/#how-to-add-what-you-know)
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Who this is for

Students on the **Future Leaders International Undergraduate Double Degree Program** at
Peking University — Guanghua's flagship international undergraduate initiative, now a
network of 15 partner business schools.

Cohort 7 lands in September. Cohort 6 is already here. This is written for both, and for
every cohort after.

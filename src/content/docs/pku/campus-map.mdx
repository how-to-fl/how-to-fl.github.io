Campus is about a kilometre and a half top to bottom and it is genuinely easy to get lost in,
because most of it looks like a park and the buildings are named after people you haven't
heard of yet. This is the version with only the things you need on it.

<CampusMap />

## The shape of it

The campus is **walled**, with gates. That matters more than it sounds: you can't cut across
the boundary wherever you like, so a building 200 m away as the crow flies can be a ten-minute
walk if the nearest gate is behind you.

**Weiming Lake** (未名湖) sits in the middle with **Boya Tower** on its bank. Everyone uses
them as the reference point — "north of the lake" is how people give directions, so it's worth
knowing which side of it you're on.

The thing that surprises most of us: **you don't live inside the walls.** Zhongguanyuan Global
Village is just outside the east side, and your walk to class every morning goes in through a
gate.

## Where you'll actually be

**Zhongguanyuan Global Village** (北京大学中关新园) — home for both years. No.126 Zhongguancun
North Street. About **five minutes' walk from the east gate**, and about **a kilometre from the
teaching buildings**, which is a genuinely pleasant walk in October and a grim one in January.

**Guanghua School of Management** — where nearly all your classes are. There are two buildings
next to each other, No.1 and No.2, and your timetable will say which. Most Future Leaders
teaching happens in **Building 1**.

**The New Sun Student Center** (新太阳学生中心) — the one building you'll visit for reasons that
have nothing to do with studying. The International Students Division is on the **3rd floor**,
and this is where you register when you arrive, collect your campus card, and sort out anything
involving your visa. As of 2025 the layout was:

| | |
|---|---|
| Ground floor + B1 | New-student registration, on arrival day |
| 3rd floor | International Students Division |
| Room 324 | Visa and residence permit agent service |
| Room 330 | The stamp you need to claim medical costs back |
| Basement | Photo studio — for the passport photos every form wants |

Those room numbers were still right as of August 2026. Check anyway if something's moved —
but they're the ones to head for.

## Getting in and out

**East Gate of Peking University** (北京大学东门) on **Line 4** is the station, and it comes up
right by the east gate — which is also the gate nearest Global Village. It's the one you'll use
almost every time.

The **West Gate** is the famous red one everybody photographs. It's beautiful and it is on the
wrong side of campus from you; the walk from the metro is long.

## Why this map and not the official one

PKU publishes a lovely illustrated campus map, and we haven't reproduced it here. Two reasons:
it's PKU's artwork rather than ours to republish, and it's drawn for charm rather than accuracy
— the buildings sit where they looked good on the page. Ours is drawn from OpenStreetMap, so
the distances are real and the scale bar means something.

What the official map *is* excellent for is names: it's the authoritative list of what every
building is called in both languages. That's where the Chinese names above came from. You can
download it from the [International Students Division](https://www.isd.pku.edu.cn/), and it's
worth having on your phone for the bits of campus this map leaves out.

## Something missing?

If there's a building you needed and couldn't find, that's a gap worth closing — tell us and
we'll add it. Same rule as the [Beijing map](/map/): we look coordinates up rather than
guessing them, so a rough "it's the one behind the second canteen" is a perfectly good report.

## Global Village — where you'll live

Rooms are **reserved for FL students**, so unlike most exchanges you don't have to find
anywhere to live. That's a bigger deal than it sounds.

It's officially 中关园/中关新园 (Zhongguanyuan Global Village), just off campus — across the
road rather than on it — and about **ten minutes' walk from the Guanghua lecture halls**.

<ul class="fl-stats">
  <li><span class="n">10 min</span><span class="l">walk to Guanghua</span></li>
  <li><span class="n">~20</span><span class="l">rooms share a floor kitchen and laundry</span></li>
  <li><span class="n">3×</span><span class="l">a week, corridors and bathrooms cleaned</span></li>
  <li><span class="n">¥0</span><span class="l">rent — the scholarship covers it</span></li>
</ul>

**Your first move on arrival is the reception desk in the front building.** That's check-in.

### The room

A single room in a two-person flat — you get your own bedroom, and share a small living
room and bathroom with one other person. Comparable to a decent European hall of residence.
**Building 4's rooms have all been renovated**, so if you get a say in where you land, that's
the one to ask for.

There's **no kitchen in the flat**, but there are shared kitchens and washing machines on
each floor, between about twenty rooms. Corridors, common rooms and bathrooms get cleaned
three times a week; **your own room is yours to clean.**

### What else is in the complex

Global Village is several buildings, not one. Among them:

- **Canteens** right in the complex, doing breakfast, lunch and dinner — which is why a lot
  of us barely cook
- A **small supermarket**, and a PKU hotel
- A **leisure centre** with a small gym, a swimming pool, a sauna and bowling lanes
  (Kang Le Recreation Center, B1 of Building 4) — the refurbishment is finished and the pool
  and sauna are open again
- The **Learning Commons** on the 2nd floor of Building 5 — group study rooms, a quiet
  self-study space, a conference room for 60–70, and a library of books on Chinese history
  and culture in English and Chinese
- A **mailroom** — it sends you a notification slip when something arrives. Bring photo ID
  to collect

And the thing nobody tells you: because every FL student lives here, **everyone you know is
suddenly a neighbour**. That shapes the year more than any single facility.

## Your campus card

This is your ID, your library card, your canteen wallet and your building access, so sort
it early.

**Activate it at B1-117, New Sun Student Center** (新太阳学生中心). The Campus Card Centre is
the same place — 010-62767858.

**Topping up:** Alipay or WeChat, both of which need to be working first.

- *WeChat:* search 北京大学校园卡 → 智慧校园 → follow the prompts
- *Alipay:* search 校园卡 → 支付宝校园卡 → choose 北京大学

**Your consumption password** is the last six digits of your passport number. If any of
those six are letters, it's `888888` instead; if only the last character is a letter, use
the six digits before it. You'll be asked for it above **¥30 in a single meal**.

**Lost it?** Lock it immediately, at a transfer machine or the Card Centre. A replacement is
¥40 — but if it stops working within three months and isn't visibly damaged, they'll
replace it free.

## Getting in and out of campus

Entry and exit are by **facial recognition**. If it doesn't recognise you — which happens —
there are three fixes:

1. Take your campus card to the guard room and register there
2. The WeChat mini-program 北大人脸采集 — log in and take the photo
3. Room 1158, 1st floor, No.1 Science Building, with your card

### Getting visitors onto campus

PKU is a tourist destination in its own right and access is controlled, so **your friends and
family cannot simply walk in** — which is a nasty surprise the morning your parents arrive.

You book them in yourself: open the **PKU app**, search **"Appointment"**, and follow it
through. Do it **before** they turn up at the gate, not while they're standing at it. Visitor
slots can run out in peak season, so for a visit you've planned around, book as early as the
system lets you.

## Getting online

PKU **prepays your internet for September and October**; after that you pay it yourself at
`its.pku.edu.cn` (网费充值).

Two networks: **PKU** and **PKU secure**. Connecting isn't enough — without logging in at
`its.pku.edu.cn` you only reach PKU's internal network. This covers Global Village too.

Stuck? Computer Service Center, Room 1158S, ground floor of Natural Science Building 1 —
010-62751023, its@pku.edu.cn.

### Your two email addresses

You get **both**, and you need to watch both:

- **`studentID@stu.pku.edu.cn`** — the main one. Most university and FL team mail lands here
- **A Guanghua address** — school-specific things go here instead

The PKU mailbox is 10GB while you're a student, dropping to 500MB after you leave — so pull
anything you want to keep before you go.

### The PKU VPN — not the one you're thinking of

Separate from a personal VPN. **PKU's own VPN (`vpn.pku.edu.cn`, Pulse Secure)** gets you
into the library's databases and journals from off campus. Install it early; you'll want it
the first time you try to work from your room and hit a paywall.

## Places you'll actually use

- **PKU Library** — 8 million books, 4,000+ seats, card swipe to get in, one-month loans.
  Help on 010-62761003
- **Guanghua Library** — Bloomberg (12 terminals in Guanghua 303 and 305B), Wind, Capital
  IQ. **Physical access only**, so you have to go in
- **Booking a study room:** WeChat mini-program, search 北大空间 "Beidakongjian", log in with
  your campus ID. Needs about **three people** to justify a booking — and **cancel if you
  don't use it**, or your booking credit drops and you get locked out for a while
- **Printing:** basements of buildings 45 and 29; Guanghua Library 3rd floor (ask at the
  desk to set up an account); 3D printing at the University Library, 010-62759145
- **Post:** post office near the ICBC branch; parcels at 近邻宝 Jinlinbao near the South
  Gate; or the Global Village mailroom
- **Phone shops:** China Mobile at B1, dorm 45A · China Unicom near Zhongguancun subway ·
  China Telecom near the Southeast Gate
- **PKU Hall** — 2,167 seats, concerts, film, lectures, **cheap tickets with your PKU ID**.
  `pku-hall.com`
- **Souvenir shop** — B1, New Sun Student Center, 9:00–18:30. You will want the merch; PKU
  kit gets genuine double-takes elsewhere in China

Weiming Lake and the gardens are the nicest part of campus and cost nothing. Go early, or
in the snow.

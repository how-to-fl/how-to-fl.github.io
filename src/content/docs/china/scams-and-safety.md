**We haven't written this one yet.** Until we do, the two things worth knowing:

- **The airport taxi scam is the one you'll actually meet**, and it's covered properly on
  [getting in from the airport](/china/airport-and-taxi-scams/).
- **96110 is the national anti-fraud hotline.** Phone and message fraud is the common one —
  nobody legitimate will ever ask you for a verification code or a transfer. If a call is
  frightening you into paying something, that is the tell.

Beijing is a genuinely safe city to walk around in, which is the honest headline. What's
missing below is the specific cons that target new foreign arrivals — the tea house, the
bar bill, the art student. If one has been tried on you, that's exactly what belongs here.

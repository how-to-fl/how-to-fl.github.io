You need a Chinese bank account. Not "it would be handy" — **your stipend cannot be paid
without one**, and the programme won't start paying until the account exists and is registered.
So this is a first-fortnight job, not a someday job.

## It has to be ICBC or Bank of China

This is the bit people get wrong by being efficient. Whichever bank a friend recommends, or
whichever branch is closest, **scholarship registration only accepts an account at the
Industrial and Commercial Bank of China (ICBC) or Bank of China (BoC)**. Any branch in the
mainland is fine — it doesn't have to be the one by campus.

Once it's open, you send the **account opening slip** to the Future Leaders team, and from
about mid-September you link the card number to your PKU ID. Miss that and the money simply
doesn't arrive.

## What to bring with you

- **Your passport.** Non-negotiable, for opening an account and for changing money.
- **Your admission notification**, or another proof you're enrolled — some branches ask.
- **A Chinese mobile number.** Some branches won't open an account without one, which makes
  the SIM card the thing you do first.
- **The "Account Opening Instructions for International Students"** — as of 2025 the
  International Students Division issued this and the bank expected to see it. Pick it up from
  the ISD on the 3rd floor of the New Sun Student Center before you go.

Two things worth asking the staff to do while you're sitting there, because doing it later
means going back:

- **Apply for a UnionPay card** with the account. It's the card that works everywhere; a
  foreign Visa or Mastercard is the one that doesn't.
- **Have them bind the account to WeChat Pay and Alipay**, install the bank's own app, and turn
  on WeChat payment notifications (微信提醒功能).

Expect it to take a while. Every account we know of took longer than anyone budgeted for.

## Where the banks are

There's an **Agricultural Bank of China on campus**, and ICBC, Bank of China, Bank of Beijing
and China Construction Bank all sit within a walk of the gates. They're on
[the campus map](/pku/campus-map/) and on [the Beijing map](/map/) with coordinates, which is
more reliable than a walking time.

We're saying that carefully because the two official booklets disagree with each other. *Get
Ready for PKU* puts ICBC 20 minutes from the East Gate; the *International Student Guide* puts
it one minute away. They can't both be right, and neither is worth trusting over a map.

The hotlines they agree on, and most have English service:

## Bringing money in

**Cash you can carry in: RMB 20,000.** That's a hard limit — anything above it isn't allowed
through. There's no limit on foreign currency, but **carrying more than USD 5,000 in cash means
declaring it to customs**, and not declaring it is a problem rather than an oversight.

Bring some cash anyway. Everything here runs on the phone, and for the first day or two — before
Alipay and WeChat Pay are working with a Chinese card — cash is what you've got.

**Change money at banks, not anywhere else.** You'll need your passport, there's a daily limit
for non-Chinese citizens, and rates move through the day and differ between banks. For a large
amount, ask in advance: some branches want an appointment and some can't handle your currency
at all.

Traveller's cheques are accepted by many Beijing banks and take **over a month** to clear, which
makes them useless for anything you need soon.

## Cards from home

Mastercard, Visa, Amex, Discover, JCB and Diners Club all work in major cities and larger shops.
Most ATMs take Maestro, Visa (Plus) and Cirrus.

**Check your daily withdrawal limit with your home bank before you fly** — it's a five-minute
call that saves an unpleasant afternoon.

One specific trap: when you pay a PKU bill online at `studyatpku.com`, the payment page offers
to charge you in your own currency or in RMB. **Choose your own currency**, and know that
foreign cards carry a fee that domestic UnionPay cards don't:

| Card | Fee |
|---|---|
| Domestic UnionPay | 0% |
| International UnionPay, Visa, Mastercard | 1.8% |
| American Express | 2.0% |
| JCB, Diners | 2.6% |

Editing files on your laptop — commit when you're done

Coordinates have to be WGS-84. Amap (高德) and Baidu use a deliberately offset system, so anything you copy from them lands 300–500 m from where you meant. Grab coordinates from openstreetmap.org — right-click the spot, "Show address", read the numbers out of the URL. Not sure? Leave them blank and describe the place instead; we'd rather fill in a gap than chase a wrong pin.
